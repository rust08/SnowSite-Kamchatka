### Final strict evidence contract
Strict requires four scoped source kinds: `access_verification` claims exact network ID/version/hash, site access node, location, intra-site mm and truck hash; `capacity_measurement` claims availableCapacityM3; `screening_allow` claims decision, ruleId, scope, reviewer and date; `operational_status` claims active. Each record declares siteId, horizon, validFrom/validUntil, checkedAt and claims object. Record `sourceRefs` must include all four refs; `operationalEvidenceRef` names the last. Reviewer date and checkedAt cannot be in the future of asOf. Tampering with site capacity or node without updating the supplied evidence is rejected. Caller controls evidence and cannot claim authenticity; a trusted operator validation process remains required. The positive strict fixture is synthetic and not a real site. Polygon remains explicitly unsupported; v4.4 adds partial A08a below.

### v4.4 A08a internal roundtrip distance
`accessVerification.intraSiteDistanceMm` is retained; positive values can only enter strict model if `intraSiteDistanceBasis:"roundtrip"` is explicit in record AND scoped access claims. Those claims must also include `accessProfileHash=hash(context.accessProfiles??null)` in addition to prior exact distance, truck, node, location and network claims. Adapter emits `intraSiteRoundtripMm` plus `intraSiteDistanceEvidence` carrying matched value, basis, horizon, date, network/truck/profile hashes. Untyped positive records are still refused rather than guessed/doubled. Legacy zero without basis remains backward-compatible (road-only). Exploratory can explicitly supply the new distance and matching evidence in assumptions; it never copies historical/verified distance to a conditional node without scope. Input assumptions remain in provenance. Actual site survey, source access connector, geometry and operational/legal validation remain external; polygon work is still open.

### v4.5 — optional local GeoJSON boundary (A08b)
SiteRecord may carry `geometry: {type:"Polygon",coordinates:[outerRing, ...holeRings]}` and `geometryEvidenceRef` (known source, included in sourceRefs). Geometries are WGS84 `[lon,lat]` in exact 1e-7 degree grid, no silent rounding. 1–16 rings and at most 512 positions total including closures. Rings must be closed, contain distinct vertices (except closure), have nonzero signed area, no self-intersection/backtracking. Either winding is accepted. Holes must be strictly inside outer ring and must not touch, cross or nest. Validation uses BigInt planar orientation, exact segment intersection, and winding number for containment, O(V²) bounded. No MultiPolygon, 3D, CRS override, pole or longitude-spanning >=180° geometry; these fail explicitly, not repaired. Schema enforces structure; runtime additionally checks topology, shared resource budget and precision. This is local planar topology, not a geodesic/cadastral survey.

In strict adapter the source must also be `kind: site_geometry`, scoped to the same site, horizon, validity and exact `claims.geometry`. Hash is over the supplied representation (not a canonical topological identity), not a spatial deduplication proof. Exploratory mode retains conditional access/capacity rules and needs_review. Geometry remains in the catalogue; provenance of adapted site carries geometryHash, geometryEvidenceRef, geometryUse=metadata_only. Access location/node and internal roundtrip evidence remain separate; an entrance may lie outside the polygon, and no area, legal status, road connectivity or capacity is inferred. No real records have been promoted. Example in `tests/v45-geometry.test.mjs` (synthetic).
